About Me

My name is Víctor Soria Pardos and I am a SoC Interconnect Architecture Engineer at Openchip and a Teaching Assistant at Universitat Politècnica de Catalunya (UPC). I received my Ph.D. in Computer Architecture from the UPC and the Barcelona Supercomputing Center (BSC-CNS). I also hold a Master in Innovation and Research in Informatics and a BSc from the UPC and Universidad de Zaragoza, respectively.

My expertise includes several topics of computer architecture. Specifically, focuses in CPU microarchitecture, parallel computing, cache coherence, synchronization, and domain specific accelerators. I've published in top-tier computer architecture conferences, including ISCA and MICRO. I've colaborated in different RISC-V open-source CPUs (Lagarto, DVINO and Sargantana).
